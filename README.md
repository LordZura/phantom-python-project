# The Wolrd Beyond

## description
this is a simple text based game, you start the game and have to choose from 3 classes, swordsman, archer or horseman, and you will recieve specific items and randomized stats (still depends on your class) and what you can do for now, is things like traveling to different countries and settlements, and travel, while traveling you spend days, you might encounter bandits, you will be forced to fight. theres also traven, where you can find market, sell items, or recruit troops. you can enter tournaments and beat 9 opponents to win a price of 5000 gold (but i recommend you get stronger first). speaking of getting stronger, you can increase your stats by training, but to train, you need skill points, which you can get from experience as you deal damage and kill enemies, which you can do on encounters while traveling (sadly only way for now, ill add more in future). you can also do simple things like check your inventory or stats.
